# Roadmap

Where Nera is going, roughly in order. Dates aren't promised: Nera has one maintainer.

**Now: first public release**
- Public repository, signed and notarized releases on GitHub, signed updates.
- An independent security review.

**Next**
- Reproducible builds you can verify.
- Remove Chromium's remaining background requests to Google services.
- Safe Browsing through a privacy relay, so Google doesn't see your IP address.
- More of the engine contract on Chromium (see checklist 005).

**Later**
- End-to-end encrypted sync between your Macs.
- iPhone and iPad.
- Translations.

Ideas and votes: [Discussions](https://github.com/tariq235/nera/discussions).
